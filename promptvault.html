Where to keep this file

This is one file and it holds your prompts in whatever browser you opened it in. So open it from the same place every time, or you will find an empty vault and think you lost them.

iPhone or iPad. Save it to Files, then open it once and add it to your home screen from the share menu. Tap that icon from then on.

Android. Save it to your files, open it with Chrome, then Add to Home screen.

Laptop. Put it somewhere you will not tidy away, then bookmark it. Dropbox or iCloud is grand, it still opens straight off the disk.

Different browser means a different vault. That is not a bug, it is what having no account costs. Use the backup above to move between them.
