What actually turns up?

Plain files. The four tools are single HTML files. You save one, you tap it, it opens in your browser like any other page. Nothing to install, no internet needed, no account. The two skills are folders with a SKILL.md inside, which you drop in your skills folder and Claude picks them up.

Do I need to be able to code?

No. You need to be able to save a file and tap it. Nothing here has a build step or a terminal in it. If you do want to change something, it is all readable English and one file, so you can point Claude at it and say what you want different.

Where does my stuff get saved?

In your own browser, on your own device, and nowhere else. There is no server, no login, no sync and no tracking, so nothing you type ever reaches me or anybody else. The flip side is that clearing your browsing data wipes it, which is why the vault has a backup button. Use it.
