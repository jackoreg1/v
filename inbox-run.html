    The four moves

    Every draft in the demo came out of the same four moves. The setter applies them to every thread it reads.

    Stage 1

    Pick it back up

    Get a dead thread breathing again. No pitch, just a human answering the message they left hanging.

    Start where the lead spoke last and never got a reply. That filter is where the 106 came from.

    Ah sorry, only seeing this now. How’s the training been going since?

    Done when: the lead replies.

    Stage 2

    Ask about the training

    Find out if they fit on behaviour, not demographics.

    One behaviour question per message, never two. Days trained, lifts moving, mirror moving. Is a dad predicts nothing.

    Fair play, four days a week is no joke. Are the lifts going up but the mirror’s not showing it?

    Done when: they confirm the pattern themselves.

    Stage 3

    Let them say it

    The lead names the problem and the cost in their own words.

    Reflect back exactly what they told you, then ask what they have tried. Their stated reason, their stated deadline. That move has closed a lead before.

    So the week is grand and the weekend undoes it. What have you tried to sort that side of it?

    Done when: they show intent.

    Stage 4

    Put a time on it

    A booked call. Nothing else gets decided in the DMs.

    Offer the call and only the call. No price, no programme detail, all of that gets sized on the call in their own numbers.

    Honestly this is easier talked than typed. What day this week suits you for a quick call?

    Done when: a day and time is in the calendar.

    The lines that never get crossed

    • The whole thread gets read before a word gets drafted, first message to last.
    • One question per message, never two.
    • Max one emoji, and none is grand.
    • No price and no programme detail in the DMs. That is the call’s job.
    • Qualify on behaviour, not demographics. Is a dad predicts nothing.
    • A hold stays held until its unlock date.
    • A mate, a peer or a stop never gets pitched. A reply is owed a human reply with nothing riding along.
    The setter drafts, I check every one, you read and you send. Nothing goes out on its own.
