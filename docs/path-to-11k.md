# 11,200 a month, what it actually takes

Jack named the number on 29 Aug 2026. This is the arithmetic, not a plan. The
only uncertain input is his own price, which is still not settled.

## At the current price, the number is 15

At 4,500 for six months, that is 750 a month per client.

| Six month price | Per month | Clients needed for 11,200 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **4,500** | 750 | **14.9** |
| 6,000 | 1,000 | 11.2 |
| 9,000 | 1,500 | 7.5 |
| 12,000 | 2,000 | 5.6 |
| 15,000 | 2,500 | 4.5 |

**Fifteen concurrent six month clients, delivered by one man, is not realistic.**

So 11,200 at 4,500 a head is not an effort problem, it is a pricing problem. The
same number at 9,000 needs seven and a half clients, which one man can carry.

**This is the real reason the unsettled pricing question matters.** Whether
4,500 is the six month total or the upfront at 20 percent off is the difference
between needing 15 clients and needing 8.

## Audits alone will not do it either

At a grand each, 11,200 a month is **11.2 audits a month, 2.6 every week**, each
with a 48 hour turnaround, on top of doing the ringing that finds the next one.

That is the promise breaking, exactly as flagged in `docs/offer-field-sales.md`.

## Blended, holding 4,500

| Clients on the six months | Recurring | Audits still needed a month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | 3,000 | 8.2, about two a week |
| 6 | 4,500 | 6.7 |
| **8** | **6,000** | **5.2, about one a week** |
| 10 | 7,500 | 3.7 |
| 12 | 9,000 | 2.2 |

**Eight on the six months plus roughly one audit a week** is the first shape of
this that a single person could actually deliver.

## What is not knowable yet, and must not be modelled

How many calls make an audit, and how many audits make a six month client.
**Nobody knows, because zero strangers have ever paid Jack.**

So do not build a timeline off invented conversion rates. Run twenty calls,
count what happens, then the arithmetic above turns into a date. Until then any
month by month plan is fiction.

## The honest order

1. **One stranger pays a grand.** That is the only number that matters this
   month. It has never happened.
2. **Settle the price**, because 11,200 is unreachable at 750 a month and
   comfortable at 1,500.
3. **Twenty calls, counted**, so the conversion rates stop being a guess.
4. Then 11,200 becomes a date instead of a wish.

11,200 is not the next decision. **The first grand is.**
