## Why the grand is an easy yes, and it is not a guess

Run through the Rep Burn Map's own maths on three invented solar outfits, the
grand comes out at well under two percent of the problem already on the board:

| Outfit | What it is costing him a year | The grand is | The 4.5k is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5 reps | EUR 77,250 | 1.29% | 5.83% |
| 8 reps | EUR 207,000 | 0.48% | 2.17% |
| 20 reps | EUR 526,800 | 0.19% | 0.85% |

Inputs invented to see the shape, not a claim about any real company.

**So never name the grand before the number is on the board.** The price is
only small in comparison to something. Say it cold and it is just a grand.

**My call, not yours:** do not make the grand ask if his total comes in under
about 20,000. Below that the grand is five percent of his problem and it reads
as dear. Take the call as a relationship and move on.

## The number to lead with is burn, not the total

Running the maths on all three scenarios, **`gapReal` is always the biggest
line** by a mile. It is also the softest, because it is the gap between his best
rep and his middle rep, and every owner has a story about why that gap exists.

`burn` is the smallest of the big lines and by far the hardest to argue with.
It is reps he hired, paid and lost, and he told you the numbers himself two
minutes ago.

So on the phone:

- **Lead with `burn`.** "That is four lads you paid for who never sold a thing.
  Five weeks each, at six hundred a week. Eighteen grand."
- **Hold `gapReal` for the plan.** It is the biggest prize and it belongs in the
  paid work, not in a cold call where it sounds like a stretch.
- **Never lead with the total.** A total sounds like a pitch. One number he
  recognises sounds like the truth.

## What the grand buys, in his words

"If you want, I can look at it for a grand."

- He gets a **PDF of what is being looked at**, so he knows what he is buying
  before Jack disappears for two days.
- **48 hours later**, a plan of action and the prompts to run it.

The prompts are the part nobody else is selling him. Jack is the AI and systems
person for a sales floor, so the deliverable is not a report, it is a plan plus
the thing that executes it.

## What goes in the PDF

The scope, drawn off the same eight things the burn map already asks about, so
what is looked at matches what was measured on the call:

1. The hiring ad itself, and what it tells a rep about the job
2. The first fourteen days of a new rep
3. Who actually trains, and what they train off
4. What the best rep says, against what the middle rep says
5. The scoreboard, or the absence of one
6. The not now pile and what happens to it
7. Where the leads come from and what they cost
8. How the reps are paid, and what that pay makes them do

## The six months, EUR 4,500

Already built, it is the map in `rep-burn-map.html`:

1. Put a scoreboard up
2. Fix the first fourteen days
3. Take your best rep's words
4. Work the not now pile
5. Stop hiring people who cannot sell
6. Make it hold without you

Eighteen moves under those six, in full detail. **The fixes ship in full.** Jack
decided that against the advice and it is not up for revisiting.

## Two things not settled, ask Jack before quoting either

1. **Does the grand come off the 4,500, or is it on top?** Total is either 4,500
   or 5,500 depending, and that has to be straight before he says it on a call.
2. **Is 4,500 the monthly run or the upfront?** The older agreed shape was
   priced two ways, monthly recurring or the whole thing upfront at 20 percent
   off. Read one way, 4,500 is the upfront and the monthly is 5,625, which is
   937.50 a month. Read the other way, it is 750 a month and the upfront is
   3,600. Both are defensible, they are very different offers.

## The risk worth naming

**The 48 hours is a promise that gets harder the better outreach goes.** Three
grands taken in one week is three plans inside 48 hours each, on top of ringing.
That is a good problem, but it is the one that will break first, so decide the
cap now rather than when he is already late for somebody who has paid.

## The measuring stick

Zero strangers have ever paid Jack. **The grand is the thing that changes that
number**, not the 4,500. Everything upstream of the grand exists to get to the
grand.
