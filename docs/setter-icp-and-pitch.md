# Setter: who it is for and how it gets sold

Companion to `setter.html`. The page is the product. This is who pays for it,
what can honestly be said to them, and what the first three messages are.

Written 29 August 2026 against the Track Record page in Notion. Every claim in
the outreach section below traces to something in the Proven column. Anything
that did not trace is not in here.

## Two ICPs, do not mix them up

There are two and they get confused constantly.

1. **The lead ICP.** Who the coach should be talking to. That one lives inside
   the tool, on the ICP tab, and the coach edits it. Defaults are behaviour
   based, straight off the Josh work.
2. **The buyer ICP.** Which coaches will actually pay for this. That is this
   document.

---

## The buyer ICP

The tool only earns anything if there is a backlog of unanswered DMs sitting in
the account. No backlog, no product. So the gates are about their inbox, not
about them.

### Gates, all five have to be true

| Gate | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Sells **online** coaching, not just floor sessions | A gym floor PT fills from the floor. Nothing arrives in the DMs to leak. |
| Roughly **2,000 followers or more, and posting** | Below that the inbox is empty and there is nothing to find. |
| **Does their own DMs**, no setter and no agency on it | If someone is already paid to do this, you are selling into a job that exists. |
| The sale happens **on a call**, not through a checkout link | A booked call has to be worth something or the whole thing pays nothing. |
| Programme is **€300 or more** | Under that a booked call is not worth the hour it takes to get it. |

### The tell, same shape as the leak zone filter

The buying signal is visible from outside the account. Open their profile and
count. **Story replies and comments they never answered.** Three or more in a
fortnight and there is a pile behind the door. That is the evidence brief, and
it takes four minutes to gather.

Same principle as a repeated Google review complaint. One unanswered message is
a busy week. Twenty is a leak.

### The cut. One of these and walk away

- **Trains Josh's exact people in Ireland.** Jack works for Josh. Selling this
  to his direct competitor in the same niche and the same country is not a
  grey area. This gate goes first because it is the one that would be easiest
  to talk yourself out of.
- Already pays a setter or an agency for DMs.
- Sells a low ticket app or a €40 plan only.
- No inbound at all, so there is nothing for the tool to work on.
- Wants it built out for them for free first.

## Where they come from, in order

The decision made on 1 August 2026 was **warm intros through Red Pick Media,
not cold outreach.** That decision still stands unless it gets changed on
purpose, so:

1. **Red Pick Media sub-accounts.** Coaches already inside the same white label
   Josh sits in. Warmest door there is and it needs one conversation, not a
   list.
2. **Josh's own network.** Coaches he knows and is not competing with. Costs
   nothing to ask, and only after his own invoice is settled.
3. **Cold, last.** Only if the two above are exhausted. Three touches, then
   stop, same as always.

"The rest of the PTs in Ireland" is a market, not a plan. The plan is the three
rungs above, in that order.

## The offer

The page on its own is a hard sell. It is a file. What sells is the run.

> **The inbox run, then the tool.** Jack reads the whole inbox once, hands back
> the list of people who messaged in and never got an answer, and sets the tool
> up in their voice so they can work the list themselves from then on.

The list is the proof and it is generated before any opinion is offered. Same
move as the evidence brief: show them their own numbers, do not describe a
service.

**Pricing.** €500 for the first three, €800 after. Half up front through the
Stripe link, same rule as everything else. The tool goes with it and is theirs
to keep. Never build the run before the deposit lands.

## The three touches

Lowercase subject, vague, curious. Sixty to ninety words. Sells the next step
and nothing else.

**Touch 1, the door.** Never a call.

> howya [name], had a look at your account and counted [N] story replies from
> the last fortnight with no answer on them. not a criticism, everyone doing
> their own DMs has the same pile. I did this for a coach in july, read the
> full inbox and found 106 people who had messaged in and never got a reply.
> two of them were warmer than anyone on his actual lead list. want me to send
> you what I found on yours?

**Touch 2, day 3.** New angle, never a bump.

> [name], one more thing on this. the ones that cost the most are not the cold
> ones, they are the people who already replied to you once and got left. they
> were interested enough to type. do you know roughly how many of those are
> sitting in yours?

**Touch 3, day 6.** Claim becomes proof, and only if the thing exists.

> last one from me. I built the tool I use for this, it works out how you write
> from your own messages and drafts the next one in your voice. two minutes to
> show you. want a look, or will I leave you at it?

Then stop. Three is the limit.

## The 15 minute call

Ask, then be quiet.

1. How many people message you in a month?
2. What is a client worth to you over the whole thing?
3. Of the people who message, how many end up buying?

Then size it out loud in their numbers, not yours. Softened to counts if the
euro figure cannot be proven. Then the offer, then a yes, a no, or a named
date. A no gets "what would have made that a yes". Every answer sharpens the
next ten.
