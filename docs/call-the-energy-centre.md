# Call card, The Energy Centre

One page. Hold it while it rings.

## Before you dial

Open [the rep ad](https://to.indeed.com/aak2ln4slnvt) or
[the canvasser ad](https://to.indeed.com/aazlf4lfyt7c) and **take the phone
number off it**. That number exists so reps can ring it, so nobody is screening
you.

Ask for **whoever runs the sales floor**. Not HR, not reception.

## The opener

> "Saw you were hiring reps. How many did you take on this year, and how many
> are still with you."

Then **stop talking.** Do not explain who you are until he asks.

Not "again". You have not counted the repeat postings.

## You only need three numbers

You already know the fourth off their own ad. **A rep costs them about 900 a
week** before selling anything. 650 gross for a canvasser, plus the van, the
phone and the fuel card.

So ask him three things, conversationally, not as a list:

1. **How many did you take on this year?**
2. **How many were gone inside three months?**
3. **How long before a new fella makes his first sale?**

## The maths, on the spot

**Gone, times weeks, times 900.**

| Gone | Ramp | That is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | 6 weeks | 16,200 |
| 4 | 8 weeks | 28,800 |
| 6 | 8 weeks | 43,200 |
| 8 | 10 weeks | 72,000 |

Say it back traced to his own words:

> "So that is six lads you paid for who never sold a thing. Eight weeks each,
> at about nine hundred a week with the van and the fuel. That is forty three
> grand gone."

**Lead with that number. Not the total, not the best rep gap.** The best rep gap
is bigger but he has a story about it. This one he cannot argue with, he just
told you the numbers.

## The ask

Only if the number is over about 20,000. Then:

> "If you want, I can look at it properly for a grand. You get a PDF of exactly
> what I am looking at, and I come back inside 48 hours with a plan and the
> prompts to run it."

Then **shut up.** First one to talk loses.

## Three things he might say

- **"Sure that is just the industry."** "It is. That is why it is worth
  forty three grand to be the one shop it is not."
- **"What would you actually do?"** Do not give the six months on the phone.
  "That is exactly what the grand buys, and you will have it Thursday."
- **"Send me something."** No. "I could, but it would be generic. Give me the
  grand and it is about your floor, or leave it and I will ring you in a month."

## Never say

- Anything from their Indeed reviews. Not the 1 out of 5, not the CEO approval.
  It is anonymous, it is insulting, and it ends the call.
- The word AI, unless he says it first.
- The 4,500. That is the upsell after the grand is delivered, not on this call.

## After

Write down what he actually said, the three numbers and what he did. **Twenty of
these counted is what turns 11,200 a month from a wish into a date.**

The 4,500 price is still unsettled, see `docs/path-to-11k.md`. **It does not
block this call.** Settle it before the grand converts, not before you dial.
