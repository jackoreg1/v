# The field sales outreach loop

Jack moved his target market onto owner run field sales operations on 29 Aug
2026. Every other niche buys admin fixing, which he learned. A sales floor buys
sales fixing, which is what he actually is. He has a sales background. That is
the whole reason for the pivot, so never drift the targeting back to trades,
dentists, gyms or salons.

## Who

Owner run field sales operations, 5 to 30 reps. Solar, heat pump, broadband,
alarms, insulation. Ireland, Cork and Munster first.

Not the enterprise sales floor with a VP of Sales. The fella who owns it, sells
in it, and trains the new lads himself when he gets a chance.

## The lead source, which Jack found himself

**A company advertising the same rep role over and over on Indeed is publicly
announcing its churn.** That is the signal. Nobody re-posts the same field sales
job four times in a year because things are going well.

Two things make it better than a bought list:

- The churn is evidenced before you ever speak to him. You are not guessing.
- **The ad carries a phone number somebody is paid to answer.** There is no
  gatekeeper problem, the number exists so that reps ring it.

How to work it:

1. Search Indeed Ireland for the rep roles: "sales representative", "field
   sales", "canvasser", "solar sales", "energy advisor".
2. Look for the same company and the same role posted more than once in twelve
   months. Note the dates and how many times.
3. Note the fleet size if the ad says it, the area, and what they sell.
4. That count is your opener's evidence. Hold it, do not lead with it.

## The opener, his words, do not improve them

> "Saw you were hiring reps again, how many did you take on this year and how
> many are still with you."

It is a **question, never a pitch.** That is the whole design. It gets him
talking about his own number instead of listening to yours. Nothing gets sold on
this call.

Do not open with the pitch, the credentials, the tool, or the word AI. If a
draft message or call opener does any of those, it is off pattern, rewrite it.

## What his answer tells you

You are listening for one thing: does he already know his own number.

- **He knows it and it is bad** ("took on twelve, four left"). Best case. He has
  already done the arithmetic and it hurts. Go to the Rep Burn Map.
- **He does not know it.** Almost as good. The not knowing is the problem.
  "That is the thing most lads cannot answer off the top of their head." Offer
  to work it out with him, which is the Rep Burn Map.
- **He knows it and it is fine.** Believe him and move on. Do not talk him into
  a problem he does not have. Ask who else he knows in the trade and get off the
  phone politely.
- **He is guarded or busy.** Get a time, not a promise. "When is quiet for you,
  before the dinner or after?"

## Where it goes

The Rep Burn Map is the next step, not the pitch. Run it with him, on the phone,
using `rep-burn-live`. His own numbers doing the persuading is the point. Jack
never argues a prospect into it.

## Rules

- **A question, never a pitch**, on the first call and in every first message.
- **Numbers do the persuading, not adjectives.** Every claim carries a figure.
- **Never inflate.** His own model runs an honesty cap and a mean multiplier.
  Copy that goes the other way is off voice and it is also bad selling here,
  because the man on the phone knows his own trade better than you do.
- Irish, spoken, plain. Short sentences. No em dashes.
- **Jack rings and Jack sends. Claude never contacts a prospect.**

## The measuring stick

Zero strangers have ever paid Jack. That is the number this skill exists to
change.

So at the end of any piece of work here, say plainly whether it moved a stranger
closer to paying, or only felt like progress. Building a better list is not
progress. A call that happened is.

One action at the end, not three.

## What is not settled yet

Flag these rather than inventing an answer:

- **What he actually charges a field sales owner.** The agreed shape is a 0 to
  15k profit runner priced two ways, monthly recurring or the whole thing
  upfront at 20 percent off. The figures behind it are not written down where a
  phone session can read them, and `assessment-business/offer-and-pricing.md`
  still records trades as the lead niche from 19 July, so it contradicts this
  pivot and has not been corrected.
- **What happens between a good call and money.** Not defined. If a call goes
  well and Jack asks what next, say it is undefined and help him decide it
  there, do not invent a funnel he never agreed.
