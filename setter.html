The framework

Seven rungs. You never skip one. Each message sells the next rung, never the coaching.

    Never

    • Never two questions in one message. They answer the easy one and the real one is gone.
    • Never price in the DMs. Price with no context is just a number to say no to. It goes on the call.
    • Never pitch a peer, a mate or a coach. Different conversation, and pitching burns it.
    • Never a fourth touch. Three and you stop. More burns the inbox and your name with it.
    • Never "just bumping this". If you have nothing new to say, you have nothing to send.
    • Never message off a list without opening the thread. Half the time they already answered you.
    • Never book a call in the first message. Cold, that moves them one step, not four.
